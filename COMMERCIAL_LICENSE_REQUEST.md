# Commercial License Request

Literal — Deterministic MCP Harness Protocol may be evaluated under the repository license. Any commercial, production, customer-facing, hosted, embedded, resale, consulting, or revenue-generating use requires prior written authorization.

## Required Information

Send a commercial license request with:

- Company legal name
- Billing country and operating regions
- Legal contact name and email
- Technical contact name and email
- Intended use case
- Deployment model: on-premises, private cloud, hosted service, embedded product, or other
- Expected number of environments, agents, users, and tool calls per month
- Data sensitivity and compliance requirements
- Desired license start date and term
- Support expectations
- Whether source modifications are required

## Evaluation To Commercial Conversion

A proof-of-concept may not be moved to production until the commercial agreement is signed. The commercial agreement can cover production use, redistribution, support, custom terms, and optional private enhancements.
